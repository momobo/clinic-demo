Delegation Plan

Modes: Automation = AI does it, I check. Augmentation = I and AI work it together. Agency = AI operates with latitude inside boundaries I set.

| # | Capability | Plan (original) | Mode | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Empathy | brainstorming, with the help of claude chat | Augmentation | I decide who the users are and which constraints to drop; the AI asks questions and proposes defaults. |
| 2 | Design | using claude code to help to a detailed design, produce a testing plan | Augmentation | The AI drafts requirement, tests and rules; every number is my decision. |
| 3 | Architecture | mostly from claude, but ask for confirmation | Augmentation | The AI proposes, I push back (hosting, update speed, cost) and the design changes. Changed from the original idea, which was closer to Automation. |
| 4 | Implementation | claude code | Agency | The AI builds from prompts I approve, inside my constraints, a test suite and "done when" criteria. |
| 5 | Judgement | testing using the testing plan in phase 2 | Augmentation | The AI runs the tests, but I decide whether the product is good (e.g. the phone lag, "too barebone"). |
| 6 | Shipping | implement and explain to the personnel (human) | Augmentation | The AI prepares the steps and checks; I do the outward-facing actions (push, publish, passwords) and explain to the staff. |
