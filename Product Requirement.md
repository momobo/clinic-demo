The Clinic Wait Time Project, Part 2 - Step 1: Product Requirement

A patient standing in the clinic's parking lot, on a basic smartphone and a mobile data connection, scans a QR code and sees the current wait as a range (for example "20–35 min") in under 3 seconds, without installing an app or creating an account. The range is never wider than 15 minutes, and is based on the live pace of the queue, not only on past history. The patient can check the wait from anywhere, but can take a number only on site, with one tap; they then see their position and updated range, refreshed within 30 seconds of any change to the queue. A banner on the page tells them when their turn is near (replacing SMS in v1). They can cancel with one tap; the cancelled ticket is dropped and cannot be reinstated, and everyone behind them sees a shorter wait. The phone remembers its ticket through a browser token; if it is lost, staff can find the ticket by number. A staff member, logged in to a separate staff page with a shared password, can open the queue in under one minute, mark the next patient as served in at most two taps, remove a patient, and move a patient to the front. The system stores only a ticket number, a join time and a status. It stores no names, phone numbers or health data, and keeps anonymous timestamps for 90 days to improve the forecasts. A patient sees only their own number and the "now serving" number. There is one queue, and the clinic is assumed to have a staff member present whenever it is open.
